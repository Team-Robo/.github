## 🚀 About Us

**Team Robo (Robo@EEE)** is a student-led robotics team from the School of Electrical & Electronic Engineering at **Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore**.

We build full-stack intelligent robotic systems integrating **AI, 3D perception, 6-DoF grasping & manipulation, human–robot interaction, and autonomous navigation**.

---

## 🏆 Competitions

- 🤖 **IEEE ICRA BARN Challenge** — Benchmarking Autonomous Robot Navigation in complex and dynamic environments ([2024](https://github.com/Team-Robo/mlda-barn-2024) · [2025](https://github.com/Team-Robo/mlda-barn-2025) · [2026](https://github.com/Team-Robo/the_barn_challenge_robo)).
- 🏠 **RoboCup@Home** — Developing autonomous domestic service robots for speech interaction, person perception, navigation, and mobile manipulation.

---

## 🔬 What We Do

- **6-DoF Grasping & Manipulation** — Targeted segmentation (YOLO / SAM 3), F2G-Pose shape completion, GraspGenX & AnyGrasp synthesis, MoveIt 2 / MTC motion planning
- **Computer Vision & Perception** — Human pose, body Re-ID, face recognition, identity fusion, 3D tracking, cabinet & seat occupancy perception
- **Human–Robot Interaction** — Offline on-device speech recognition (`whisper.cpp`) and CPU TTS (`Piper` / `sherpa-onnx`)
- **Autonomous Navigation & Mapping** — OpenUSD-to-SLAM semantic mapping, FAR Planner (`move_base`), and Optuna BARN hyperparameter tuning
- **System Tooling & UI** — Multi-machine ROS 2 node lifecycle scheduling, web dashboard, synchronized bag recording, and robot face display
