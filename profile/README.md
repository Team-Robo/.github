# 🤖 Team Robo

**Robotics Team under NTU School of EEE**

---

## 🚀 About Us

Team Robo is a student-led robotics team from the School of Electrical & Electronic Engineering at Nanyang Technological University (NTU).

We focus on building intelligent robotic systems that integrate **AI, perception, and autonomous navigation** to solve real-world challenges.

---

## 🏆 Competitions

We actively participate in international robotics competitions, with our main focus on:

* 🤖 **IEEE ICRA BARN Challenge**
  Benchmarking Autonomous Robot Navigation in complex and dynamic environments.

* 🏠 **RoboCup@Home**
  Developing service robots capable of assisting humans in domestic environments.

---

* Embedded systems & robotics integration
